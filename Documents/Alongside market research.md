# PART 6: HOW TO VALIDATE BEFORE BUILDING

## The Risk
You've spent weeks on documentation. What if users don't want it?

## Validation Steps (BEFORE Full Build)

### Step 1: Landing Page Test (2 weeks)

Create a simple landing page that describes Alongside:
- "Fitness coaching for people fitness culture forgot"
- Email signup for early access
- Share in target communities

**Success metric:** 500+ email signups indicates genuine interest

### Step 2: Survey Existing Audience (1 week)

If you have access to:
- College students
- Teacher networks
- ADHD communities

Survey them:
1. Have you abandoned a fitness app? Why?
2. What would make you stick with one?
3. Would you pay for [describe Alongside]?

**Success metric:** 60%+ say they'd pay or try

### Step 3: Beta Programme (4-8 weeks)

Before public launch:
- 50-100 beta users
- Free access
- Weekly feedback calls
- Track actual retention (not stated intent)

**Success metric:** 20%+ still using after 30 days

### Step 4: Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

Don't build everything. Build:
- Onboarding (name, one goal, one condition)
- Daily check-in (energy, mood)
- One programme (Hip Recovery - you have it)
- One Coach personality (Steady)
- Basic progress view

**Success metric:** Users complete sessions and return

## Pricing Strategy Recommendation

### Option A: Classic Freemium (Your Current Plan)
- Free: 4 sessions/week, 1 programme
- Personal: £9.99/mo or £89/yr

**Risk:** May cannibalise paid users

### Option B: Time-Limited Trial
- 14-day full access
- Then £7.99/mo or £69/yr

**Risk:** Users don't convert from trial

### Option C: Pay What You Can (Novel)
- Free tier always available
- "Supporter" tier: £3-15/mo (user chooses)
