## 2. What's already decided — don't re-derive this

The taster code mechanics are fully specified already, in `alongside_beta_engagement_strategy_20jun2026_v1.docx` Section 6. Read it in full before writing anything. Headline facts any comms referencing taster codes must get right:

- One unique code per organisation — codes differ per org specifically so engagement is trackable per community
- Unlocks 1 month full Personal-tier access from the date of **first redemption**, not from when the code was issued
- Redeemable any time in Year 1 (to 30 Nov 2026) — no pressure to use it immediately
- No card required, no charge, automatic reversion to free tier after the month
- At reversion, a single non-intrusive in-app prompt appears — **copy already drafted**, don't rewrite it from scratch: *"No pressure at all. If you have 30 seconds, we'd love to know what would make Alongside work better for you."* — optional, skippable, no follow-up if skipped.

**Redemption data feeds "five in the pound" investment decisions and gets shared back with organisations** (per the beta strategy doc's follow-up engagement programme) — worth knowing since it affects what the welcome/explainer copy should set expectations for ("your organisation will see anonymised engagement, not individual data").

## 3. The one hard constraint — read this before COMMS-4

**Journal Privacy Rule is absolute, no exceptions.** Journal content is never subject to signal detection or used to personalise anything — feeling-word selector and mood history only. COMMS-4's whole premise ("how engagement data personalises email content") must be designed around this from the start, not bolted on after. If a personalisation idea would require reading journal content to work, the answer is no, not "find a workaround."

## 4. Voice and tone — established, don't reinvent

- Coach voice throughout any in-app or coach-attributed copy: **Nurturing only, permanently.** No other tone exists or is planned at any tier.

## 5. Social media — new scope, needs its own decisions

Not covered by the existing COMMS tasks, added per this brief. Needs answers to, not just content:

- **Which platforms?** Not yet decided in any doc found — check current channel presence (LinkedIn personal profile mentioned as a live task elsewhere on the schedule; no confirmed org-level presence found).
- **Timing relative to org outreach** — a taster code only exists once an organisation has agreed to share it (per the beta strategy's outreach sequence). Social posts about taster codes shouldn't go out generically before that, or they'll promise something not yet available to anyone reading them. Sequence this explicitly against the outreach timeline in the beta strategy doc, don't treat social as independent of it.
- **Per-org vs. general framing** — since codes are org-specific, most of this content is probably "here's what to share with your community" material *for organisations to post*, not Alongside's own general-audience social content. Worth deciding which is actually being asked for before drafting.
