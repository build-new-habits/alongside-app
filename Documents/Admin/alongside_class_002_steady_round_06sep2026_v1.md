# Alongside: Move — Class 002: Steady Round
## 06 Sep 2026 v1

Build New Habits | Strand: **trunk strength**. Format: **bodyweight circuit**. Intensity: **gentle**.

The second written class, chosen because it should strain the shape Class 001 set. Where it did and did not is recorded at the end.

---

## What this class is for

**A circuit that never asks you to go faster.** That is the whole design problem. A circuit is a format built on pace and count — rounds, reps, a clock — and every one of those is a way of falling behind.

So this one keeps the *structure* of a circuit, which is genuinely useful — you know what is coming, it repeats, it ends — and takes out the racing.

🔴 **No clock is shown. No reps are counted aloud. There is no round two "target".** You move for a while, then you stop for a while. Twice.

⚫ **The strand is trunk strength, and the honest thing about trunk work is that it is boring and it works anyway.** The class says that rather than dressing it up.

| **Equipment** | none |
| **Flags** | floor position · getting up and down repeatedly · holding positions |

### Flags, stated up front on the card

> **What's in it:** five movements, twice through · lying on your front and back · getting up and down between them · one hold
>
> **Go at your own pace.** Sit any movement out — the round still counts. There is no clock and nothing to keep up with.

🔴 **"Getting up and down between them" is the flag that matters here** and it is the one a fitness app would never think to give. For somebody with a hip, a knee, or fatigue, **the transitions are the hard part, not the movements.** Naming it means they can decide before they are on the floor for the third time.

---

## The shape

Five movements. Through twice. A long stop in the middle and a long stop at the end.

**Every movement has a stated stopping point that is not "when you can't do any more".** That phrasing is the standard in this format and it is exactly wrong here: it makes the end of the set a failure event.

## The script

`[SCREEN]` is what appears; `[VOICE]` is what is said. `⏸` is silence, held.

---

### 1 · Before we start (1 min 30)

`[SCREEN]` **Five movements, twice through.**
No clock. Sitting one out still counts as doing the round.

`[VOICE]` This is a circuit, so it repeats — five movements, and then the same five again. Knowing what's coming is most of the point.

`⏸ 8s`

`[VOICE]` There's no timer on screen and I'm not counting reps at you. You'll move for a bit, then stop for a bit.

`⏸ 10s`

`[VOICE]` If you sit one out, the round still counts. I mean that structurally, not kindly — there's nothing keeping score.

`⏸ 12s`

---

### 2 · Glute Bridge (2 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Glute Bridge** — on your back, knees bent. Lift, hold a moment, lower.
Stop when it stops feeling smooth.

`[VOICE]` On your back, knees bent, feet flat. Push through your heels and let your hips come up. Hold it a second at the top. Down slowly.

`⏸ 20s`

`[VOICE]` Again, in your own rhythm. I'm not counting.

`⏸ 40s`

`[VOICE]` Stop when it stops feeling smooth. **Not when it starts feeling hard — when it stops feeling smooth.** Those are different, and the first one is the useful signal.

`⏸ 25s`

**Exercise:** `glute-bridge` · **sit-out:** yes · **stop cue:** smoothness, not failure

---

### 3 · Dead Bug (2 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Dead Bug** — opposite arm and leg, slowly.
If your back lifts off the floor, you've gone far enough.

`[VOICE]` Stay on your back. Arms up towards the ceiling, knees bent above your hips.

`⏸ 12s`

`[VOICE]` Lower one arm behind you and the opposite leg away. Slowly. Then back.

`⏸ 30s`

`[VOICE]` Here's your limit, and it's a real one: if your lower back lifts away from the floor, that's the end of the range. Come back a bit. That's not caution, that's where the work is.

`⏸ 40s`

**Exercise:** `dead-bug` · **sit-out:** yes · **stop cue:** back leaves the floor

---

### 4 · Bird Dog (2 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Bird Dog** — hands and knees. Opposite arm and leg.
Slow beats far.

`[VOICE]` Onto your hands and knees. Reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back.

`⏸ 20s`

`[VOICE]` You're trying not to tip. That's the whole exercise — the reaching is just what makes it hard not to.

`⏸ 30s`

`[VOICE]` Slow beats far. Every time.

`⏸ 40s`

**Exercise:** `bird-dog` · **sit-out:** yes · **standing route:** no

---

### 5 · Plank (1 min 30)

`[SCREEN]` **Plank** — forearms, or hands, or on your knees.
Come down before it gets ugly, not after.

`[VOICE]` Down onto your forearms. Knees on the floor is a real version of this, not a lesser one.

`⏸ 15s`

`[VOICE]` Hold it while it's good. **Come down before it gets ugly, not after** — the shaking-and-sagging bit isn't extra credit, it's just the exercise stopping and you carrying on.

`⏸ 35s`

`[VOICE]` Down when you're ready.

`⏸ 15s`

**Exercise:** `plank` · **sit-out:** yes · **easier route:** knees

---

### 6 · Hip Hinge (2 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Hip Hinge** — standing. Push your hips back, chest towards the floor.
Only as far as your back stays long.

`[VOICE]` Stand up — take your time getting there.

`⏸ 15s`

`[VOICE]` Feet about hip width. Push your hips backwards and let your chest come towards the floor. Your back stays long the whole way.

`⏸ 30s`

`[VOICE]` The moment your back starts to round, that's your range for today. It moves about. That's normal.

`⏸ 40s`

**Exercise:** `hip-hinge-drill` · **sit-out:** yes · **standing** · ⚫ *placed fifth deliberately — one transition, at a point where getting up is a rest rather than a demand*

---

### 7 · The stop (2 min)

`[SCREEN]` **That's one round. Sit down for a couple of minutes.**
Round two is the same five.

`[VOICE]` That's the five. Sit down, or lie down.

`⏸ 20s`

`[VOICE]` Round two is exactly the same. Nothing gets harder and there's no target.

`⏸ 40s`

`[VOICE]` If one round is what today had in it, stop here and that's a whole session. I'd rather you did one round for six weeks than two rounds once.

`⏸ 40s`

🔴 **The exit is offered in the middle, out loud, before anybody has to invent it.** In every other circuit format, leaving after one round is quitting. **Here it is a named, ordinary way to finish**, and it is said by the coach rather than reached by the person on their own.

---

### 8 · Round two (5 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Same five, same order.**
Glute Bridge · Dead Bug · Bird Dog · Plank · Hip Hinge

`[VOICE]` Same five, same order. I'll say less this time — you know what they are now.

`⏸` *then the five, cued by name only, with the same holds*

⚫ **Round two is quieter on purpose.** Repeating the full coaching would say the person had not learned it. Naming the movement and going quiet says they had.

---

### 9 · Leaving (1 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Done.** Take your time getting up.

`[VOICE]` That's it. Two rounds, or one — either way you did the session.

`⏸ 10s`

`[VOICE]` Trunk work is boring and it works anyway. That's most of what there is to say about it.

`⏸ 8s`

`[VOICE]` See you next time.

## What this class did to the contract

**It held.** `beats` took a circuit without changing shape. Three additions, all of which Class 001 would have needed eventually:

```
rounds          2          // repeat count for the beat range
roundRange      [2, 6]     // which beats repeat
stopCue         string     // per movement: what tells you to stop
easierRouteId   string?    // per movement, distinct from seatedAlternativeId
```

🔴 **`stopCue` is the important one.** Every movement here ends on a stated signal — *smoothness*, *your back leaves the floor*, *before it gets ugly*, *while your back stays long*. **Never "when you can't do any more".** That phrasing makes the end of a set a failure event, and it is the default in this entire format.

⚫ **`seatedAlternativeId` and `easierRouteId` are not the same field**, and Class 001 could not have shown that. Knees-down plank is not a seated version — it is the same movement, less of it. Collapsing them would have made "can't get to the floor" and "can't hold it long" the same problem.

🟡 **One thing Class 001 had that this lacks: a noticing beat.** A circuit does not obviously want one. **Not adding it for symmetry** — if a third class wants one, the field is there.

---

## Open

🟡 **The middle stop is two minutes and that is a guess.** Too short and round two is grim; too long and the session dissolves. Only reading it aloud with a stopwatch will tell.

🟡 **Third class should serve a strand neither of these touches** — `pacing` or `getting-going` — and be mindfulness-led, before the contract is fixed.
