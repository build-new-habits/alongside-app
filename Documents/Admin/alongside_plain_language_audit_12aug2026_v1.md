**`AMRAP` and `EMOM` are already glossed on first use** — *"Complete as many rounds as possible (AMRAP)"*, *"Every Minute On the Minute (EMOM)"*. They are defined terms, not jargon. v1 flagged them wrongly; later reuse of the acronym is correct once defined.
