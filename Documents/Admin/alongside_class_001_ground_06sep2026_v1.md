# Alongside: Move — Class 001: Ground
## 06 Sep 2026 v1

Build New Habits | Strand: **trusting your body again**. Format: **stretch and mindfulness**. Intensity: **gentle**.

The first written class. Everything else follows the shape this sets, so it is written in full rather than as a template.

---

## What this class is for

**Not to stretch you.** The movements are a vehicle. The point is fifteen minutes of moving and noticing that nothing went wrong — repeated, until that stops being a surprise.

Somebody working on *trusting your body again* has usually had the opposite experience: a movement that hurt when it should not have, or a body that stopped being predictable. **You cannot argue somebody out of that.** You can give them small, slow, entirely survivable evidence, and let them collect it.

⚫ **So the class never says "see, that was fine".** It asks what they noticed and leaves the answer with them. The person draws the conclusion or they do not, and either is allowed.

### Flags, stated up front on the card

> **What's in it:** getting down to the floor and back up · lying on your back · a few minutes of paying attention to how your body feels
>
> **Go at your own pace.** Sit any part out. Sitting out is not skipping — it is a way of doing the class.

🔴 **Body-focused attention is a real flag, not a formality.** For some people — after trauma, during a flare, with certain eating-disorder histories — being asked to notice bodily sensation is not neutral. **It is named on the card so nobody meets it mid-class**, and every noticing beat below offers an external alternative.

## The script

**Convention:** `[SCREEN]` is what appears; `[VOICE]` is what is said. Where they differ, that is deliberate — voice carries pacing, screen carries what you need if the sound is off. `⏸` is silence, held.

---

### 1 · Arriving (2 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Get comfortable on the floor.** Lying on your back, knees bent, feet flat. A chair works just as well.

`[VOICE]` Find somewhere to lie down, if that's available today. On your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor. If the floor isn't happening — and some days it isn't — a chair is completely fine and nothing after this changes.

`⏸ 8s`

`[VOICE]` Let your hands rest wherever they land. You don't have to do anything with them.

`⏸ 10s`

`[VOICE]` No breathing instructions yet. Just let it be whatever it already is.

`⏸ 15s`

⚫ **Deliberate: no breath control in the opening.** *Trusting your body again* often comes with a history of being told to breathe correctly. **Instructing the breath in the first minute makes the body a thing to be managed, which is the opposite of this class.**

---

### 2 · The first noticing (1 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Where are you touching the floor?** Not how it feels. Just where.

`[VOICE]` I'd like you to notice where your body is touching the floor. Not whether it feels good or bad. Just where the contact is.

`⏸ 12s`

`[VOICE]` Heels. Maybe your hips. Shoulder blades, the back of your head.

`⏸ 15s`

`[VOICE]` If noticing your body isn't somewhere you want to go today, listen for the furthest-away sound you can hear instead. That's the same practice.

⚫ **Location, not sensation.** *Where* is answerable and has no wrong answer. *How does it feel* is an evaluation, and for somebody whose body has been unpredictable it is the harder question. **The external alternative is offered before it is needed**, not after somebody has already found the question difficult.

**Practice reference:** `feet-on-floor-grounding`, adapted to lying.

---

### 3 · Cat-Cow (2 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Cat-Cow** — on hands and knees, or seated in a chair.
Move slowly. Stop anywhere that says stop.

`[VOICE]` Come onto your hands and knees when you're ready. Or stay in the chair and let your spine do the same thing sitting up.

`⏸ 10s`

`[VOICE]` Let your back round upward. Slowly. Then let it drop the other way. There's no shape you're trying to reach.

`⏸ 20s`

`[VOICE]` If anywhere says stop, stop there. That's not you failing the movement — that's the movement telling you where it goes today.

`⏸ 25s`

`[VOICE]` Six or seven of those. In your own time.

**Exercise:** `cat-cow` · **sit-out point:** yes · **seated route:** yes

---

### 4 · Thread the Needle (2 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Thread the Needle** — one arm under the other, both sides.
Only as far as it goes today.

`[VOICE]` From hands and knees, slide one arm underneath the other, and let that shoulder come down towards the floor.

`⏸ 12s`

`[VOICE]` You're not trying to get anywhere. Go until you feel it, and then stay there.

`⏸ 20s`

`[VOICE]` And back. Other side when you're ready.

`⏸ 25s`

**Exercise:** `thread-the-needle` · **sit-out point:** yes · **seated route:** `thoracic-rotation-seated`

---

### 5 · The second noticing (1 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Anything different?** There might not be. That's an answer too.

`[VOICE]` Come back to lying, or sitting. Whatever you started in.

`⏸ 10s`

`[VOICE]` Is anything different from four minutes ago? It might not be. That's a real answer, not a failed one.

`⏸ 20s`

🔴 **"There might not be" is load-bearing and must survive any edit.** A question that only accepts *yes, better* teaches people to report improvement. **This class is collecting evidence, and evidence you have coached is worth nothing.**

---

### 6 · Knees side to side (2 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Knees side to side** — slow, small, both directions.
Small is the point, not a compromise.

`[VOICE]` On your back, knees bent. Let them fall slowly to one side. Only as far as they want to go.

`⏸ 15s`

`[VOICE]` Back through the middle. And the other side.

`⏸ 20s`

`[VOICE]` Small is fine. Small is the point. You're not measuring anything.

`⏸ 25s`

**Exercise:** `thoracic-rotation` (supine variant) · **sit-out point:** yes

---

### 7 · Stillness (3 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Nothing to do for three minutes.**
Eyes closed if you want them closed.

`[VOICE]` Let your legs go wherever they're comfortable. Arms wherever they land.

`⏸ 10s`

`[VOICE]` Eyes closed if that's alright. Open is completely fine.

`⏸ 30s`

`[VOICE]` Nothing to do now. You've done the moving part.

`⏸ 60s`

`[VOICE]` If your mind's gone somewhere, that's what minds do. You don't have to bring it back.

`⏸ 60s`

⚫ **Eyes closed is offered, never instructed.** For a lot of people it is the least safe part of a class like this.

---

### 8 · Leaving (2 min)

`[SCREEN]` **Take your time getting up.**
Roll to one side first if that's easier.

`[VOICE]` When you're ready — and there's no rush — roll onto one side, and come up from there.

`⏸ 15s`

`[VOICE]` That's it. Fifteen minutes of moving, and your body did all of it.

`⏸ 8s`

`[VOICE]` See you next time.

🔴 **THE CLOSING LINE IS THE WHOLE CLASS.** *"Your body did all of it"* is a fact — it is not a compliment, an interpretation, or a claim about how it went. **It states what happened and leaves the meaning to the person.** Anything warmer — *well done*, *you should be proud* — takes the conclusion off them, which is the one thing this strand cannot afford.

## What the data contract needs

Written from what this class actually used, not invented first:

```
id            "class-ground-001"
title         "Ground"
strands       ["trusting-body", "at-home-in-body", "self-kindness"]
formats       ["stretch", "mindfulness"]
intensityBias "gentle"
durationMins  15
position      "floor"
seatedRoute   true
equipment     []
flags         ["floor-transfer", "eyes-closed-offered", "body-focused-attention"]
beats         [ { kind, screen, voice, holdSeconds, exerciseId?, practiceId?,
                  sitOut: boolean, seatedAlternativeId? } ]
```

⚫ **`beats`, not `exercises`.** A class is not a list of movements — half of this one is silence and questions. **A schema shaped like a workout would have thrown away the parts that make it a class.**

⚫ **`seatedRoute` is a property of the whole class, not of each beat.** Somebody who cannot get to the floor needs to know that before they start, not to discover it beat by beat.
