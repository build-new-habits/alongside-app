# PART 1: YOUR LOGOS - HONEST ASSESSMENT

## Logo 1: "Build New Habits" (BNH with Seedlings)

### What Works
- **Strong metaphor**: Growth, roots, foundation - perfect for habit building
- **BNH integration**: Letters forming roots is clever
- **Tagline**: "Grow Into Your Best Self" is warm and aspirational

### Concerns
- **Complexity**: Too detailed for app icons (won't scale down well to 64×64px)
- **Dated aesthetic**: Clip-art style feels 2010s, not 2025
- **Colour palette**: The browns and greens feel earthy but not modern/tech
- **Multiple elements**: Curved text, seedlings, roots, BNH - too much happening

### Verdict
**Good concept, needs modernising.** The growth/seedling idea is strong. The execution needs simplifying for digital use.

## Logo 2: "Alongside" (Two Runners with Heart)

### What Works
- **Perfect name visualisation**: Two figures running together = "alongside"
- **Heart connection**: Shows the emotional/supportive aspect
- **Seedlings bookending**: Links to the BNH brand
- **Hierarchy**: Clear primary (icon) and secondary (text) elements

### Concerns
- **Complexity again**: Two full human figures + heart + seedlings = won't work at small sizes

- **Similar aesthetic**: Same clip-art style as Logo 1
- **Green gradients**: Nice but very common in wellness space

### Verdict
**Better than Logo 1, but still needs work.** The "two together" concept is excellent. The running might send wrong message to people who feel they "can't" run.

**Keep the concepts, modernise the execution.**

For the app, you need:
1. **Simple icon** that works at 64×64px (app icon size)
2. **Wordmark** that's clean and readable
3. **Consistent system** that works across app, web, social

# PART 2: COLOUR RESEARCH FINDINGS

## What the Research Says

### For Fitness Apps
- High-contrast colors like Nike Run Club's neons work well for quick information during intense activity. Calmer palettes like Headspace's pastels create a relaxing atmosphere.
- Dark mode options improve readability and reduce eye strain, especially for users who work out at night.

### For Mental Health/Wellness Apps
- Blue calms people down, which is perfect for meditation apps. Green represents health and balance, making it great for fitness trackers.
- We see wellness brands rely on blue for serenity, green for balance and neutrals for accessibility – going beyond the calming psychological associations, these colours communicate the concepts of nature, safety and openness.

### For Modern App Design 2025
- As we move into 2025, color schemes in app design will lean toward futuristic, tech-inspired palettes. Expect to see more neon tones, metallic, and gradient colors.
- Modern dark UIs use subtle gradients and neon-style highlights to add depth and character while maintaining legibility.
- Don't use pure black (#000). Choose dark grays, navies, or deep greens (#181A1B, #23272F, #14213D) for a softer, more elegant background that's easier on the eyes.

### Key Insight for Alongside
Dark interfaces convey premium quality while strategic color highlights create engaging user experiences that encourage continued interaction.

## Bonus: Light Mode Option

Most users prefer dark mode (70%+ according to research), but you should offer light mode too.

For either palette, the light mode would be:

| Element | Colour |
|---------|--------|
| Background | `#FAFAFA` (off-white) |
| Cards | `#FFFFFF` (white) |
| Text | `#1A1A1A` (near-black) |
| Secondary text | `#6B7280` (gray) |
| Accent | Same as dark mode |
