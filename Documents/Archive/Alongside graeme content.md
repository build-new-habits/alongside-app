| Sessions/week | 3 |
| Session length | 15-20 minutes |
| Focus | Deep core strength, spine protection |

### Key Principles

- **No crunches** - spinal flexion under load risks disc
- **Anti-movement focus** - core's job is to PREVENT unwanted movement
- **Progression through duration, then complexity** - not through adding weight

### Core Exercise Categories

| Category | Purpose | Examples |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Anti-extension | Prevent back arching | Dead bug, plank, hollow hold |
| Anti-rotation | Prevent twisting | Pallof press, bird dog, single-arm carries |
| Anti-lateral flexion | Prevent side bending | Side plank, suitcase carry |
| Hip stability | Control pelvis | Glute bridges, clamshells, fire hydrants |
