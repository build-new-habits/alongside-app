### 1. Before we start · 1 min 10

> The refusal comes first. Said at the end it would sound like an apology for not working.

- *arriving* — **Screen:** The end of the day. Sit anywhere. Nothing here needs to go well.  
  **Voice:** This one's for the end of the day. Sit somewhere you can stay for a while — a chair, the edge of the bed, the floor if that's easy.  
  <sub>then 10s</sub>
- *arriving* — **Voice:** One thing first. This isn't a sleep class. I can't make you sleep, and nothing this short can. What it can do is draw a line under today.  
  <sub>then 12s</sub>

---

### 2. Neck · 1 min 25

- *movement* — **Screen:** Neck — ear towards shoulder, slowly. Only as far as it goes without asking.  
  **Voice:** Let one ear drift towards the same shoulder. Not pulling. Just letting the head be heavy.  
  <sub>movement: Seated Neck Side Stretch (`seated-neck-side-stretch`) · stop cue: "as far as it goes without asking" · then 20s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Back to the middle, and the other side. Most of the day ends up in here somewhere.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 3. Side reach · 1 min 20

- *movement* — **Screen:** Side reach — one arm up and over. A long side, not a deep bend.  
  **Voice:** One arm up, and lean over the other way a little. You are making one side long, not folding in half.  
  <sub>movement: Seated Side Reach (`seated-lat-side-stretch`) · stop cue: "a long side, not a deep bend" · then 20s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Come back up, and the other arm.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 4. Cat and cow · 1 min 35 · *not in the shorter version*

- *movement* — **Screen:** Cat and cow — on hands and knees, or seated with hands on your knees. Round, then lift. Slowly.  
  **Voice:** On hands and knees if the floor's easy. Or stay sitting, hands on your knees. Round your back as you breathe out, and let it lift as you breathe in.  
  <sub>movement: Cat-Cow Stretch (`cat-cow`) · seated route: Seated Torso Rotation (`seated-torso-rotation`) · stop cue: "wherever it stops being easy" · then 20s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** A few more, at whatever speed your breathing sets. You're not stretching anything. You're telling your back the day is finished.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 5. Twist · 2 min 10 · *not in the shorter version*

- *movement* — **Screen:** Twist — lying down with knees to one side, or seated, turning gently. Let it be small.  
  **Voice:** Lie on your back with your knees bent, and let them fall to one side. Or sit tall and turn a little, one hand on the back of the chair.  
  <sub>movement: Supine Spinal Twist (`supine-twist`) · seated route: Seated Lumbar Rotation (`seated-lumbar-rotation`) · stop cue: "small is enough" · then 20s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Stay there for a few breaths. Nothing has to open.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Back to the middle, and the other way.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 6. Breathing out · 1 min 45

> The one technique in the class, and the one it names plainly: a longer out-breath. Nothing is claimed for it beyond what it is.

- *movement* — **Screen:** Breathe in normally. Breathe out for a little longer. That is all of it.  
  **Voice:** Now the breath. In, at whatever length it wants. And out, a little longer than the in.  
  <sub>movement: Extended Exhale Breathing (`extended-exhale-breathing`) · stop cue: "if it feels like effort, let it go back to normal" · then 15s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Eyes closed if you like. If the longer breath starts to feel like work, drop it. Ordinary breathing is fine.  
  <sub>held about 60 seconds (never sped up)</sub>

---

### 7. The day · 1 min 20

> A reflect beat that asks nothing to be concluded. Naming one thing is enough; judging it is not asked for.

- *reflect* — **Screen:** One thing from today. Just name it. It does not have to be good.  
  **Voice:** Think of one thing from today. Anything. It doesn't have to be good, and you don't have to decide what it meant.  
  <sub>then 25s</sub>
- *reflect* — **Voice:** Name it, and put it down. It will still be there tomorrow if it needs to be.  
  <sub>then 20s</sub>

---

### 8. Leaving · 1 min 15

- *closing* — **Screen:** That was the end of the day. The rest is the night’s business.  
  **Voice:** That's the day put down. Whatever the night does next is its business, not a mark against you.  
  **Voice, shorter version:** That's the day put down, from the chair the whole way. Whatever the night does next is its business, not a mark against you.  
  <sub>then 20s</sub>
