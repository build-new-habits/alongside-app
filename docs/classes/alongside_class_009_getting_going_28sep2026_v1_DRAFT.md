### 1. Before we start · 0 min 50

> The class makes its case before anything moves: starting is the part that already happened.

- *arriving* — **Screen:** A chair nearby. You have already done the hardest part.  
  **Voice:** Nine minutes, and a chair nearby to hold or sit on. If getting to this point was the hard bit, that part's already done.  
  **Voice, shorter version:** A few minutes, and a chair nearby to hold or sit on. If getting to this point was the hard bit, that part's already done.  
  <sub>then 8s</sub>
- *arriving* — **Voice:** Some days this wakes you up and some days it doesn't. Both count the same. We're not chasing a feeling.  
  <sub>then 8s</sub>

---

### 2. Marching · 1 min 20

- *movement* — **Screen:** March on the spot, holding the chair. Or sitting, lifting one knee then the other.  
  **Voice:** Hold the back of the chair and march on the spot. Or sit, and lift one knee, then the other. Easy pace.  
  <sub>movement: Supported Standing March (`chair-supported-march`) · seated route: Seated Marching (`seated-marching-cardio`) · stop cue: "an easy pace you could talk at" · then 15s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Keep going. A pace you could talk at. Nothing to catch up with.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 3. Reaching · 1 min 15

- *movement* — **Screen:** Reach up and over to one side. Then the other. Arms long, not strained.  
  **Voice:** Stand or sit tall. One arm up and over to the side, then the other. Long arms, nothing forced.  
  <sub>movement: Seated Side Reach (`seated-lat-side-stretch`) · stop cue: "long, not strained" · then 15s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Side to side, as slowly as you like.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 4. Turning · 1 min 20

- *movement* — **Screen:** Turn your upper body one way, then the other. Hips stay facing forward.  
  **Voice:** Arms crossed on your chest, or hands on your hips. Turn your shoulders one way, back to the middle, and the other way.  
  <sub>movement: Seated Torso Rotation (`seated-torso-rotation`) · stop cue: "only as far as it turns easily" · then 15s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Only as far as it turns easily. The first few are always the stiffest.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 5. Sit to stand · 1 min 30 · *not in the shorter version*

- *movement* — **Screen:** Sit to stand — from the chair, and back down slowly. Hands on your thighs if that helps.  
  **Voice:** Sit near the front of the chair. Stand up, and sit back down slowly. Use your hands on your thighs if that helps — that still counts.  
  <sub>movement: Sit to Stand (`sit-to-stand`) · seated route: Seated Marching (`seated-marching-cardio`) · stop cue: "stop while the next one would still be easy" · then 15s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** A few more. Stop while the next one would still be easy — that's the right place to stop, not a giving-up place.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 6. Up on your toes · 1 min 05 · *not in the shorter version*

- *movement* — **Screen:** Hold the chair. Up onto your toes, and down slowly.  
  **Voice:** Hold the chair. Up onto your toes, and lower slowly.  
  <sub>movement: Supported Calf Raise (`chair-supported-calf-raise`) · seated route: Seated Calf Raise (`seated-calf-raise`) · stop cue: "as high as is comfortable" · then 10s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** A few more, at your pace.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 7. Leaving · 1 min 40

- *reflect* — **Screen:** Anything different? Maybe, maybe not.  
  **Voice:** Anything feel different from when we started? Maybe. Maybe not. Either answer's fine.  
  <sub>then 12s</sub>
- *closing* — **Screen:** You got going. That was the whole point.  
  **Voice:** You got going. That was the whole point, and it's done.  
  <sub>then 12s</sub>
