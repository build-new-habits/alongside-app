### 1. Before we start · 0 min 55

> Said before the first movement, so a difference between sides arrives as expected rather than as a worry.

- *arriving* — **Screen:** Ankles and feet. Sitting down. One side may feel different from the other — that is common.  
  **Voice:** This one's ankles and feet, and most of it is sitting down. Shoes off if you can.  
  <sub>then 8s</sub>
- *arriving* — **Voice:** One side will probably feel different from the other. That's very common, and it's information, not a problem. We're not trying to make them match today.  
  <sub>then 10s</sub>

---

### 2. Circles · 1 min 15

- *movement* — **Screen:** Ankle circles — one foot off the floor, slow circles each way.  
  **Voice:** Lift one foot a little off the floor and draw slow circles with it. One way, then the other.  
  <sub>movement: Ankle Circles and CARs (`ankle-mobility-circles`) · stop cue: "only as big as moves easily" · then 15s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Now the other foot. Notice whether it's different. Just notice.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 3. Alphabet · 1 min 20

- *movement* — **Screen:** Write letters in the air with your big toe. A few letters, then swap feet.  
  **Voice:** Write the letters of your name in the air with your big toe. Movement from the ankle, not the whole leg.  
  <sub>movement: Ankle Alphabet (`ankle-alphabet`) · stop cue: "small letters are fine" · then 15s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Swap feet. Small letters are fine. Nobody is marking the handwriting.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 4. Heels and toes · 1 min 15

- *movement* — **Screen:** Feet flat. Lift the heels, lower. Lift the toes, lower. Rock between them.  
  **Voice:** Both feet flat. Lift your heels and lower them. Then lift your toes and lower them. Rock slowly between the two.  
  <sub>movement: Seated Heel and Toe Raise (`seated-heel-toe-raise`) · stop cue: "a smooth rock, nothing forced" · then 15s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Keep rocking at your own pace.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 5. Up on your toes · 1 min 25 · *not in the shorter version*

- *movement* — **Screen:** Standing, holding the chair: up onto your toes, down slowly. Or stay seated and lift the heels.  
  **Voice:** If standing is fine today, hold the back of the chair, rise onto your toes and lower slowly. Or stay sitting and lift your heels as high as they go.  
  <sub>movement: Supported Calf Raise (`chair-supported-calf-raise`) · seated route: Seated Calf Raise (`seated-calf-raise`) · stop cue: "as high as is comfortable" · then 15s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** A few more. The lowering is the useful half, so take your time on the way down.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 6. Knee to the wall · 1 min 35 · *not in the shorter version*

- *movement* — **Screen:** Facing a wall, one foot forward: bend the knee towards the wall, heel down. Or seated, slide the foot back under the chair.  
  **Voice:** Stand facing a wall, one foot a short way in front of you. Bend that knee towards the wall, keeping the heel on the floor. Or sit, and slide one foot back under the chair until you feel the front of the ankle.  
  <sub>movement: Ankle Dorsiflexion Drill — Wall (`ankle-wall-dorsiflexion`) · seated route: Ankle Alphabet (`ankle-alphabet`) · stop cue: "heel stays down" · then 15s · can be sat out</sub>
- *movement* — **Voice:** Other foot. If one side goes further, that is the information we talked about.  
  <sub>then 35s</sub>

---

### 7. Leaving · 1 min 15

- *reflect* — **Screen:** Stand or sit with feet flat. Notice the floor under you.  
  **Voice:** Feet flat on the floor. Notice where your weight sits — more on the heels, the toes, one side. Nothing to fix.  
  <sub>then 20s</sub>
- *closing* — **Screen:** Whether this changes how you walk is a question for weeks from now, not today.  
  **Voice:** Whether any of this changes how you walk is a question for weeks from now, not today. Today your ankles got asked. That's the class.  
  <sub>then 12s</sub>
