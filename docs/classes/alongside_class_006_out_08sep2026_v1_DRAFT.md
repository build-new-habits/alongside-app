# Class 006 — "Out"

**08 Sep 2026 v1 — DRAFT for Graeme**

Serves `being-outside`. **The first class that leaves the room, and it strains the contract in five places.** Those strains are the point of drafting it now: they are structural, and they hold whatever you decide about the voice in 004 and 005.

---

## Why this strand, and not the other two

Three strands are tied on 8 aims: `hip-range`, `not-overdoing`, `being-outside`.

- **`hip-range`** is the closest thing left to what the builder already does well, and Ground already covers gentle floor mobility. Highest duplication risk.
- **`not-overdoing`** is already touched by Stopping Early, which serves `pacing` — a very close neighbour. Two classes that feel like each other is worse than one strand waiting.
- **`being-outside`** is untouched by anything, and it is **the one place a class beats a built session outright.** The builder can tell you to go for a walk. It cannot go with you.

⚫ Read its aims and it is plainly not a fitness walk: *feel more at home in my own body · have something left by the evening · wind down properly · spend more of my life outdoors · be better with the people around me · be steadier in myself when things shift.* Two of eight are about distance. **The rest are about being outside at all.**

## What this class is for

Most people who want to be outside more do not need persuading. They need it to be **fifteen minutes with a shape**, rather than a vague intention that loses to the kettle.

**This class is a walk with somebody talking occasionally.** It is not a guided meditation that happens outdoors, and it is not a workout. It gives the walk a beginning, three things to notice, and an ending — and then gets out of the way, because the outside is doing the work and the voice is not the point.

🔴 **It never tells the person the outside is good for them.** They know. Everybody knows. Saying it is the fastest way to make fifteen minutes feel like a prescription, and this strand belongs to people who have had enough of those.

---

## The script

`[VOICE]` only. **There is no `[SCREEN]` in this class** — see the contract notes.

---

### 1 · Before you go out (2 min)

`[VOICE]` This one happens outside, so before you set off — coat, shoes, whatever the weather is doing.

`⏸ 20s`

`[VOICE]` Anywhere will do. It doesn't have to be nice. A pavement is completely fine.

`⏸ 20s`

`[VOICE]` I'll say something every few minutes and be quiet in between. If you need to stop, or turn back, or take your headphones out at a road — do that. **The class survives being interrupted.** It isn't a set of instructions you can get out of step with.

`⏸ 25s`

`[VOICE]` Off you go.

🔴 **"The class survives being interrupted" is the load-bearing line of this class**, and it exists because of the one structural fact that makes an outdoor class different: **nothing here can be timed against what the person is doing.** Indoors, thirty seconds of silence is thirty seconds of the exercise. Out here it might be a road crossing, a neighbour, or a dog. Saying so at the start is what stops the person feeling they have fallen behind.

---

### 2 · The first bit (3 min)

`[VOICE]` No particular pace. Whatever your legs are doing is the right speed.

`⏸ 60s`

`[VOICE]` You don't have to look at anything yet. Just walking.

`⏸ 90s`

⚫ **Three minutes of near-silence, and the longest gap in any class so far.** Indoors, silence is something to be justified. Out here it is the default and the talking is the interruption.

---

### 3 · Something far away (2 min 30)

`[VOICE]` Find something a long way off. A building, a tree, the end of the road. Anything further away than you have looked all day.

`⏸ 40s`

`[VOICE]` Most of today has probably happened within about a metre of your face. Screens, worktops, the inside of a car.

`⏸ 45s`

`[VOICE]` Let your eyes be somewhere else for a bit.

`⏸ 45s`

**Practice:** `mindful-walk`

---

### 4 · Something under your feet (2 min 30)

`[VOICE]` Notice what you're walking on. Not how it feels about you — just what it is. Pavement, grass, gravel, mud.

`⏸ 45s`

`[VOICE]` It changes more than you'd think on a short walk. Most people stop noticing that somewhere around the age of eight.

`⏸ 50s`

`[VOICE]` If noticing your feet isn't where you want to be today, listen for the furthest-away sound instead. Same thing.

`⏸ 30s`

⚫ **The external alternative, offered the same way Ground offers it** — before it is needed, not after somebody has found the question difficult. Third class to carry it, and it is becoming a house rule rather than a per-class decision.

---

### 5 · The air (2 min)

`[VOICE]` Notice the air on the bit of you that's uncovered. Face, usually. Hands.

`⏸ 40s`

`[VOICE]` Cold, warm, damp, still. Not whether you like it. Just what it is.

`⏸ 50s`

🔴 **"Not whether you like it"** is the same instruction as Ground's *where, not how it feels*, and it matters more outdoors, because British weather invites a verdict. **The class is not asking anybody to enjoy the rain.** It is asking them to notice they are in it, which is a different and much easier thing.

---

### 6 · Heading back (2 min)

`[VOICE]` Start heading back whenever suits. There's no distance you were supposed to reach.

`⏸ 45s`

`[VOICE]` You've been outside for about a quarter of an hour. That's the whole thing — there wasn't a second part.

`⏸ 45s`

**Lighter variant line:** *You've been outside for about ten minutes. That's the whole thing — there wasn't a second part.*

---

### 7 · Back (1 min)

`[VOICE]` Whenever you get in — no rush, and no need to finish this bit outside.

`⏸ 20s`

`[VOICE]` That's it. You went outside on purpose.

`⏸ 10s`

`[VOICE]` See you next time.

🔴 **"You went outside on purpose"** is the closing, and it is the same shape as Ground's *your body did all of it*: a statement of what happened, with the meaning left to the person. **Not "well done for getting out" —** which would make going outside an achievement, and this strand belongs to people for whom it is supposed to become ordinary.

---

## The lighter variant

**Omits sections 3 and 4** — the far-away thing and the ground underfoot. Ten minutes.

What is left is going out, walking, the air, heading back, and coming in.

⚫ **The two that come out are the two that ask the person to do something.** On a bad day what remains is: put your coat on, be outside, come back. **The noticing is the part that can wait; the being outside is the part that cannot.**

Section 6's line changes, because *a quarter of an hour* is not true of a ten-minute walk.

## 🔴 What this class did to the contract — five strains, and they are the point

This is the first class that leaves the room, and it does not fit. **None of these are fatal and none should be patched quietly.**

**1 · `position` has no value for this.** The contract allows `floor`, `seated`, `standing`, `mixed`. This class is a walk. `standing` is technically true and completely useless to somebody deciding whether they can do it today. **Suggest adding `walking`** — and if a swimming or cycling class ever exists, the field is really *where the body is*, not *what posture it holds*.

**2 · There is no `[SCREEN]` anywhere.** Every other class pairs screen and voice. Here the phone is in a pocket and the person is watching traffic. The contract does not require `screen` — it is already optional — but nothing asserts that a class **without** one is deliberate rather than unfinished. **A class with no screen text should have to say so**, or the first reviewer will assume it was never written.

**3 · The timings are not timings.** `speechSeconds` indoors is a gap in a controlled room. Out here it is a guess about somebody crossing a road. The numbers are still useful — they shape the pacing — but **they mean something weaker than they do in every other class**, and nothing in the contract records the difference. This is the same distinction that split `holdSeconds` from `speechSeconds`, appearing a third time.

**4 · The safety filter does nothing here.** `classSafety()` checks every movement beat's `exerciseId` against the person's conditions. **This class has no movement beats at all**, so it passes trivially — for everybody, in every condition, including somebody who should not be walking alone today. That is not a bug in the filter; it is a class whose risks are of a kind the filter was never built to see. **Weather, light, traffic, and going out alone are real and none are in the model.**

**5 · The lighter variant may be doing two jobs again.** Same worry as Class 005's wall and step. Here, ten minutes outside instead of fifteen is a genuine lighter day — but somebody who cannot leave the house at all is not served by a shorter walk, and this class has no answer for them. **That may be correct** — a class about being outside cannot be done inside, and pretending otherwise would be the worst kind of accommodation. But it should be a decision, not an omission.

## Open questions for Graeme

1. **Is "Out" too bare a name?** It is the shortest possible and says exactly the thing. It may also look like a placeholder on a card.

2. **Going alone.** The flag names it. Nothing else in the class acknowledges that for some people — time of day, area, or history — going out alone is the barrier rather than the motivation. **Adding a line risks making it strange for everybody else; leaving it out means the class assumes a safety not everyone has.**

3. **Weather.** The class currently treats whatever is happening as fine. There is presumably a limit, and no view has been taken on where it is or whether the class should have one.
